    FAQ

    How do we start?

    Email me what you need, when you need it, and one link you like. I reply with a short plan and a start date.

    How long does it take?

    Edits: first draft in 2–3 days. Landing pages and bots: usually one to two weeks, depending on scope. I say the timeline before we begin.

    How many revisions?

    Two focused rounds are included. Extra rounds are fine — we agree on them before I start.

    What should I send?

    For an edit: footage or product shots, the offer, and a reference. For a site or bot: the goal, any copy you have, and a brand colour or two.
