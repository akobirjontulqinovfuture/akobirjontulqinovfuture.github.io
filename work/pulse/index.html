PULSEKinetic type

An event site that dances to its own beat

Open the live demo →

Overview

A kinetic-typography concept for a fictional night event. Press play and a kick drum and hi-hat are synthesised live with WebAudio; every hit stretches, lifts and thickens the letters of the title.

How it works

Change the tempo with the slider and the type follows. There are no audio files: the sound is generated in the browser. The event, artists and times are placeholders.

This is a personal concept: the brand, data and people in it are fictional.
