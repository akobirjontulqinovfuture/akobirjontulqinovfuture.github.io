Overview

A motion website for an education centre with English, IELTS and programming courses.

How it works

The hero is a domain-warped “ink & saffron” field with topographic contour lines and a lens that follows the mouse, written in raw WebGL1 with no 3D library.

This is a personal concept: the brand, data and people in it are fictional.
