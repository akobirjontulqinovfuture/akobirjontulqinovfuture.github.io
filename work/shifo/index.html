Overview

A clinic website concept where booking is the whole point. Filter by specialty, see each doctor’s next free time, pick a day on a two-week strip and a time from the free slots.

How it works

The form formats Uzbek phone numbers as you type and validates before booking. The confirmation offers a real .ics file that adds the visit to any calendar. The clinic, doctors and schedules are fictional and nothing is sent.

This is a personal concept: the brand, data and people in it are fictional.
