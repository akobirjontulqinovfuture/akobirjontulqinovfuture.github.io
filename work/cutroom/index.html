Overview

A landing-page concept for an AI video editor. Instead of a mock screenshot, the monitor plays one of my real promo edits through six live WebGL effects: chroma split, duotone, glitch cut, film grain and a liquid ripple that follows the cursor.

How it works

Switch looks and drag the strength slider — every frame is processed on the GPU in your browser. There is no product behind the page; it shows how I would present one.

This is a personal concept: the brand, data and people in it are fictional.
