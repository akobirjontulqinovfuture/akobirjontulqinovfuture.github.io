The VaultEliteToolz v2

An immersive 3D concept for elitetoolz.store

Open the live demo →

Overview

The store becomes a vault. One persistent WebGL scene lives behind the whole page, and scrolling drives one continuous camera move: through a liquid-chrome core, then down a light tunnel through six chrome gates, one for each product category.

How it works

The store’s 44 real products orbit each chamber as glass cards, then fly into an archive next to the catalogue. Quality adapts to the device (high / medium / low / no-WebGL), and all sound is synthesised with WebAudio, so there are no audio files.

This is a personal concept: the brand, data and people in it are fictional.
