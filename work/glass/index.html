Overview

A Telegram Mini App concept for ordering coffee from a fictional café. Every surface is frosted glass over moving colour, with a highlight that follows your finger, and the order bar springs up as soon as something is in the cart.

How it works

Inside Telegram it uses the native haptics and expands to full height; in a normal browser it works as a web page. It is a demo: nothing is charged and no order is sent.

This is a personal concept: the brand, data and people in it are fictional.
