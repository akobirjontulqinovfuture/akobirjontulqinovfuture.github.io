Open the live demo →

Overview

A product-configurator concept for a fictional speaker. The model is built from code — no 3D files — and lit by a studio environment generated in the browser, so the gloss, matte and chrome finishes reflect light the way real materials do.

How it works

Pick a colour, finish and grille and the model, the page colour and the headline update together. Drag to turn it, scroll to zoom; the build is remembered on your device.

This is a personal concept: the brand, data and people in it are fictional.
